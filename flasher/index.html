Klippyface Flasher

Install the display firmware from your browser. Version …

1. Pick your board

Both Waveshare boards are ESP32-S3 chips, so the flasher can't tell them apart: pick the right one.

2. Plug it in and install

Connect the board with a USB data cable, then click Install and pick its serial port. Choose erase when asked on a first install. Installing resets the board's settings either way; you set WiFi again in the next step.

Pick your board above first.

This browser can't flash boards. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop, or flash the -full.bin from the release by hand (see the guide).
Flashing needs a secure page (https). Open this flasher at luke-kearney.github.io/klippyface.

3. Then

  1. After installing, the flasher offers to connect the board to WiFi: pick your network and enter its password. (Skipped it? On your phone, join Klippyface-Setup instead, enter your WiFi and leave Server address blank.)
  2. In the Klippyface web UI, open Nodes and Accept the board under Pending

Full walkthrough and troubleshooting: Getting Started.
